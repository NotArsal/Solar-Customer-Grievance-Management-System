Customer Grievance Management System

Problem Statement:
The company currently lacks a structured system to manage customer complaints efficiently.
Issues are handled manually, resulting in poor tracking, delayed responses, and lack of accountability.

The objective is to develop a centralized grievance management system that allows customers
to raise issues and enables the company to track, assign, and resolve them systematically.

Objectives:
. Digitize and streamline complaint handling
. Improve transparency and accountability
. Ensure timely resolution of customer issues

Scope of Work:
· Design a system where customers can register complaints
· Create a ticket-based system for each grievance
. Implement role-based access (admin, employee, customer)
· Enable assignment of issues to specific team members
. Track status (pending, in-progress, resolved, unresolved)
. Maintain history and logs of all complaints

Expected Features:
. Complaint registration portal
. Unique ticket ID generation
. Status tracking dashboard
. Assignment and reassignment functionality
· Notifications/updates to customers
. Reporting and analytics (optional)

Expected Outcome:
A structured platform that improves efficiency in handling complaints, reduces delays, and enhances customer satisfaction.
